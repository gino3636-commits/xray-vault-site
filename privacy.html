Privacy Policy

Your data is yours. In plain language: X-Ray Vault is offline-first, collects no personal data, and keeps everything you create on your device.

Contents 01 · Overview 02 · Data We Collect 03 · Data You Create 04 · Sharing & Backups 05 · Device Permissions 06 · Children's Privacy 07 · Changes to This Policy 08 · Contact

Last updated: June 2026 · X-Ray Vault v3.0.40

01 Overview

X-Ray Vault is an offline-first reference toolkit for radiographers. We designed it to keep your information private: the app functions entirely on your device and does not require an account.

02 Data We Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. The app has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no user accounts. We have no servers that receive your information.

03 Data You Create

Everything you add — technique charts, positioning notes, department and fluoro protocols, OR exam references, phone directory contacts, hospital codes, licenses, CEUs, and any images you attach — is stored only in local storage on your device. It never leaves your device unless you choose to export or share it (see below).

04 Sharing & Backups

If you use the Share / Backup features, a backup file (or QR code) is created from your data and handed to the destination you pick (for example AirDrop, Files, Messages, or another X-Ray Vault install). You control where it goes; we are not involved in the transfer and never receive a copy.

05 Device Permissions

The app requests permissions only when you use a related feature, and only for on-device use:

  • Camera — to scan QR codes and capture reference photos.
  • Photo Library — to attach your own reference images.
  • Notifications — to show local reminders for license and CEU expirations. Reminders are scheduled on your device; no push server is used.

06 Children's Privacy

X-Ray Vault is a professional reference tool and is not directed to children. Because we collect no data, we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

07 Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be included in a new release of the app with a revised date above.

08 Contact

For privacy questions, please reach out through the app's support channel listed on its App Store page.
