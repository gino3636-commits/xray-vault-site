Privacy Policy

Your studies are yours. This policy explains, in plain language, what X-Ray Vault does — and doesn't — do with your data.

01 Overview

X-Ray Vault ("the app", "we", "us") is designed around a simple principle: your data should stay under your control. Wherever technically possible, the information you enter is stored on your own device rather than on our servers.

This policy describes the categories of information the app handles, how it is protected, and the choices available to you.

02 Data we collect

We aim to collect as little as possible. Depending on how you use the app, this may include:

  • App data you enter — licenses, certifications, CE hours, notes and any protocol or directory entries you add. These stay on your device.
  • Account data — if you create an account, an email address and authentication credentials.
  • Diagnostic telemetry — anonymised crash reports and performance metrics, only if you opt in.

03 On-device storage

Your entries are written to a protected, app-private area of your device's storage. Nothing is uploaded to us; data only leaves your device when you choose to share or back it up via a file, Save to Device, or a QR code.

04 Security

Because your data stays on your device, its security is tied to your device. We recommend enabling a screen lock and keeping your operating system up to date. Any backup or share files you create are under your control — store and send them only through channels you trust.

05 How we use data

We use the limited data described above solely to operate and improve the app — for example, to authenticate your account (if you create one) and to diagnose crashes. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your data for advertising.

06 Third parties

We rely on a small number of processors (for example, cloud infrastructure and crash-reporting providers) strictly to deliver the service. Each is bound by contract to protect your data and to process it only on our instructions.

07 Retention

On-device data persists until you delete it or uninstall the app. Any optional backups are retained until you remove them or close your account. Anonymised telemetry is kept for a limited period and then deleted or aggregated.

08 Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and to withdraw consent for optional processing. Because most data lives on your device, you can exercise many of these rights directly in the app. For account data, contact us using the details below.

09 Children

X-Ray Vault is intended for use by healthcare professionals and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10 Changes & contact

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be highlighted in the app. Questions about privacy can be sent to privacy@xrayvault.app or via the support page.
