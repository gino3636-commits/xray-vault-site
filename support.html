FAQ

Questions, answered.

What exactly is X-Ray Vault?
It's a radiographer's toolkit: technique & positioning references, OR & fluoro exam guides, the protocols and contacts you add, and license/CE tracking — all in one app that works offline.
Does it work without an internet connection?
Yes — X-Ray Vault is built to work fully offline. Every guide, chart, protocol and contact is available with no signal, in the OR or the basement.
How do I move my data to a new phone?
Use Share & Import: share your data as a file, save it to the device, or hand it off via QR code — then choose Import Data on the new phone to restore everything.
Is my data stored on your servers?
No. Your entries — licenses, notes, protocols and settings — live on your device. You decide when to share or back them up.
Is X-Ray Vault a diagnostic tool?
No — it's a reference and workflow toolkit, not a certified diagnostic device. Always follow your department's protocols and validated equipment.
